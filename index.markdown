This blog explores the realization of landscape and the social production of urban and territorial space, with particular attention to Lima and Peru. It also includes short articles related to the courses I teach at UNALM, as well as reflections on political economy, institutions, infrastructure and geopolitics in South America, particularly Peru, Uruguay and Chile. Across these topics, the blog examines how societies organize territory, produce institutions and power, and respond to processes of economic, technological and political change.
